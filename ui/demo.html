The approach

What makes it an agent, not a classifier.

01

Investigate, then decide

Rebuilds the whole fraud episode — walking the card's history backward and forward — and stops when the evidence settles, not at a fixed count.

02

Verify before blocking

A weak single signal gets a verification, not a block. The recommendation is recorded before and after evidence, and changes when the answer does.

03

Memory across cases

Each closed case is written back into the graph and cited by later ones — the ring case becomes evidence for the sixteen investigations after it.

04

Grounded in policy

GraphRAG retrieves the exact governing rule from the policy vector store — the disputed-charge rule surfaces first — instead of dumping raw text at the model.
